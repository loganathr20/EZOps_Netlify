Advanced DevOps Learning Path

Master reliability, monitoring, distributed systems, and large-scale automation for production-grade environments.

Why Choose the Advanced Path?

The Advanced Path is for engineers who are comfortable with Linux, automation, and CI/CD pipelines. You will learn how to operate large-scale systems reliably, manage incidents, and optimize performance.

Advanced DevOps is about predicting, preventing, and quickly recovering from real-world failures.

Graduates of this path gain confidence in managing complex infrastructure, designing SLOs, automating workflows at scale, and ensuring reliability under pressure.

Modules in the Advanced Path

Module 1

Reliability Engineering & SLOs

  • Defining SLOs, SLIs, and error budgets
  • Designing reliability metrics for services
  • Measuring uptime, latency, and performance
  • Balancing velocity and reliability in production

Go to Reliability Engineering →

Module 2

Incident Response & Troubleshooting

  • Building playbooks for incidents
  • Monitoring alerts and triage procedures
  • Debugging live production issues
  • Post-mortem analysis and continuous improvement

Go to Incident Response →

Module 3

Distributed Systems & Scaling

  • Understanding microservices architecture
  • Service discovery, load balancing, and caching
  • High availability and fault tolerance patterns
  • Cloud-native design principles and orchestration

Go to Distributed Systems →

Module 4

Advanced Automation & CI/CD

  • Pipeline optimization for reliability
  • Automated testing, canary deployments, and rollbacks
  • Infrastructure as code with repeatable environments
  • Monitoring pipelines and automating remediation

Go to Advanced CI/CD →

Tips for Advanced Learners

  • Focus on building repeatable and reliable systems
  • Simulate failure scenarios in lab environments
  • Monitor services continuously and analyze metrics
  • Document and share best practices with your team
  • Iterate on automation and CI/CD workflows
Advanced DevOps is continuous learning — anticipate issues, automate solutions, and keep systems resilient.
